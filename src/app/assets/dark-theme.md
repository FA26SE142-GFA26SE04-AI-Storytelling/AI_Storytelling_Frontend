## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an immersive, dream-inducing night mode tailored for children (ages 4–10) and parents co-reading before bedtime. The brand persona is that of an enchanted nighttime chronicler: serene, whimsical, protective, and filled with celestial wonder.

The aesthetic fuses **Ethereal Glassmorphism** with **Tactile Cosmic Playfulness**:
- Surfaces emulate deep obsidian night skies and midnight navy nebulae, drastically reducing melatonin-disrupting blue glare while maintaining wonder.
- Warm starlight and bioluminescent currents guide young eyes gently through the narrative without flashiness or sensory overload.
- Touch affordances retain comforting, toy-like tactile cues softened with starry luminescence, ambient glows, and velvety, rounded contours.
- High-contrast, non-glare typography ensures effortless phonetic tracking in dim, bedroom lighting environments.

## Colors

The dark-mode palette transforms the daytime toybox into a tranquil, starlit galaxy engineered to soothe tired eyes while sustaining storybook magic. Every chromatic choice exceeds WCAG AA (and predominantly AAA) readability standards against deep midnight bases.

### Core Architectural Layers
- **Midnight Canvas (`#090D16`):** The foundational substrate representing infinite night space. Absolute zero glare, replacing stark pure black with an indigo-tinted obsidian.
- **Obsidian Surface (`#0F1626`):** Base card and container tier providing subtle tonal separation without harsh brightness shifts.
- **Indigo Elevated (`#172038`):** Raised interactive containers, navigation shelves, and dialog cards.
- **Cosmic Border (`#283556`):** Soft, low-contrast boundary line defining containers without drawing focus away from bedtime illustrations.

### Celestial Accents
- **Starlight Amber (`#FFB84D` - Primary):** The warm, lantern-like golden glow driving interactive page progression, story creation catalysts, and reading milestones. Warm wavelength gentle on nighttime circadian rhythms.
- **Bioluminescent Teal (`#2EE6CA` - Secondary):** The pulsing light of forest sprites and magical choices. Used for affirmative actions, voice recording pulses, and audio playback indicators.
- **Astral Amethyst (`#B78CFF` - Tertiary):** Evokes magical dream-spinning and AI narration controls, parent sleep timers, and whimsical interactive surprises.
- **Celestial Sky (`#5CE1E6`) & Nebula Rose (`#FF7597`):** Supplementary story-branch identifiers and character dialogue framing.

### Night Reading Typography Spectrum
- **Stardust White (`#F0F4FC`):** Primary text color, gently warmed to prevent white-halo optical fatigue against deep navy.
- **Moonlit Silver (`#B2BDD4`):** Secondary descriptive text and prompt labels (WCAG AAA compliant on deep backgrounds).
- **Night Whisper (`#6E7E9E`):** De-emphasized indicators, timestamps, and resting state icons.

## Typography

The typography system is calibrated specifically for nighttime readability in low-light environments:
- **Comfortaa** provides round, welcoming, non-intimidating title forms that feel like soft constellation maps. Its open curves render crisply on backlit OLED displays without bleeding into surrounding dark pixels.
- **Nunito Sans** operates as the narrative workhorse. Its high x-height and generous open counters maintain legibility when display brightness is dimmed down to minimum levels before sleep.
- `story-text` features an extended `34px` line height and slightly boosted character spacing (`0.015em`) to facilitate bedtime word highlighting, finger-pointing, and visual pacing for sleepy readers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a handheld-first paradigm optimized for snug, one-handed or lap-supported bed-reading sessions.

### Grid & Responsive Mechanics
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` (20px) margins and `1rem` gutters. Interactive actions are grouped within the lower 40% thumb arc.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins, allowing simultaneous presentation of full-bleed story illustration and illuminated text narrative side-by-side.
- **Desktop / Smart TV (> 1024px):** 12-column centered layout constrained to a maximum content width of `1200px` to maintain focused bedtime storybook intimacy.

### Touch Integrity
- Interactive bedtime elements maintain a minimum target of `3rem` (48px), expanding to `3.5rem` (56px) for primary story turn and choice nodes to accommodate gross motor control in tired children.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than conventional drop shadows—which disappear or look muddy on dark backgrounds—this design system uses **Bioluminescent Aurora Layers** and **Constellation Depth Tiers**:

- **Substrate (Level 0):** Deep `#090D16` canvas with optional, extremely faint stardust noise (2% opacity).
- **Resting Story Cards (Level 1):** Solid `#0F1626` base, surrounded by a delicate 1px border in `#283556`. Outer depth is delivered via a soft, ambient indigo diffusion: `box-shadow: 0 8px 32px -4px rgba(15, 22, 38, 0.8), 0 2px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`.
- **Interactive Luminary Controls (Level 2):** Raised buttons and active choice nodes utilize vibrant backlight halos tinted with their respective accent:
  - Amber Primary: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(255, 184, 77, 0.35), 0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
  - Teal Secondary: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(46, 230, 202, 0.35), 0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Floating Sheet & Dream Overlays (Level 3):** Modal overlays use frosted Obsidian Glass (`rgba(15, 22, 38, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), framed by a luminous top-edge reflection border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Pill-Shaped (Level 3)** geometry to evoke safety, friendliness, and cloud-like softness:
- Buttons, tags, badges, and progress pills use completely rounded endcaps (`rounded-full` / 9999px).
- Main narrative panels and story choice cards feature sweeping softened corners (`2rem` / 32px), creating a plush, pillow-soft contour that feels natural for bedtime reading.
- Sub-containers, audio player controls, and inner media clips feature nested concentric radii between `1rem` and `1.5rem`.
- Sharp right angles are strictly excluded to preserve an organic, soothing visual cadence.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Starlight Hero CTA ("Turn Page", "Create Magic"):** 56px height, pill-shaped. Radiant fill in Starlight Amber (`#FFB84D`) with high-contrast Obsidian Ink text (`#090D16`). Elevated by an ambient amber starlight halo (`rgba(255, 184, 77, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Ghost Pill:** 48px height, translucent backdrop (`rgba(23, 32, 56, 0.6)`) with a 1.5px border in Bioluminescent Teal (`#2EE6CA`) and matching teal text.
- **Sleep Mode Audio Button:** Circular 48px button with Astral Amethyst border and glowing icon for sleep-timer and ambient lullaby toggles.

### Story Choice Cards
- Generous 32px rounded containers layered in Obsidian Surface (`#0F1626`).
- Framed by a 1.5px border in Cosmic Navy (`#283556`).
- When selected or hovered, the card transitions into an illuminated state with a 2px Bioluminescent Teal border and an interior ambient gradient glow (`radial-gradient(ellipse at top, rgba(46, 230, 202, 0.12), transparent 70%)`).

### Badges & Filter Chips
- Fully rounded pills (height: 32px) designed for easy tap interaction.
- Resting: Translucent navy fill (`#172038`) with Moonlit Silver text (`#B2BDD4`) and subtle border (`#283556`).
- Active: Filled with Astral Amethyst (`#B78CFF`) or Starlight Amber (`#FFB84D`) with dark text (`#090D16`), accompanied by a soft glow.

### Bedtime Prompt Input
- 54px pill-shaped search/prompt bar with a deep indigo surface (`#172038`) and a 1.5px border in `#283556`.
- Focus state activates an ambient starlight gold perimeter glow.
- Trailing voice-story button features an organic pulsating teal aura (`#2EE6CA`) indicating AI voice listening readiness.

### Checkboxes & Star Toggles
- Custom 32px rounded-full star stamps replacing mundane checkboxes.
- Unchecked: `#172038` background with a delicate `#283556` outline.
- Checked: Star fills with bright Starlight Amber (`#FFB84D`), casting a localized 12px stardust glow.

### Floating AI Sleep Narrator Bar
- Anchored 16px above the device bottom safe area.
- Designed as an illuminated frosted glass pill (`rgba(23, 32, 56, 0.9)` with 20px blur) framed in low-opacity starlight gold (`rgba(255, 184, 77, 0.2)`).
- Contains dynamic audio waveform visualizers in glowing teal and amber, with oversized touch-friendly pause/sleep buttons.
